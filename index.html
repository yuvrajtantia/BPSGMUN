Doc. No. 002 — Preamble

I. On This Chronicle

Every chronicle begins where the last left its seal. BPSGMUN returns for a third sitting — a gathering of delegates, dispatches and dissent, convened once again to test the instruments of diplomacy against the disorder of the present.

This is not merely a conference; it is a record in the making. Across six committees, delegates will draft, contest and ratify positions on questions that span courtrooms and cabinets, newsrooms and naval charts, coaching centres and constitutional crises.

“Diplomacy is the art of restraining power.”

Delegates will be asked not merely to speak, but to negotiate; not merely to argue, but to arrive — at resolutions, at compromises, at the uneasy consensus that governs real institutions. Each committee carries its own register, its own stakes, its own history.

The Third Chronicle is an invitation: to research with rigour, to debate with civility, and to leave, at the end of it, a paper trail worth keeping.
