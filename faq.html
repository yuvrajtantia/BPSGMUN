Section A — General

About the Conference

BPSGMUN — The Third Chronicle — is the third edition of the Model United Nations conference hosted by Birla Public School, Ganganagar. Delegates across six committees spend three days debating, negotiating and drafting resolutions on real and themed crises.

30th November, 1st & 2nd December 2026, at Birla Public School, Ganganagar (Chak 3HH, Suratgarh Road, Sri Ganganagar, Rajasthan).

BPSGMUN welcomes delegates across experience levels. For specific eligibility criteria for your grade or school, write to the Secretariat and they'll guide you.
